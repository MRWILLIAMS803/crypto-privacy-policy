Privacy Policy — Crypto for Beginners (TikTok Content Posting Tool)

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This privacy policy covers the internal tool used by Charles Anthony Williams Jr. ("we," "us") to publish video content to the TikTok account @charleswilliams35 via TikTok's Content Posting API.

What this tool does

This is a private, single-user publishing tool. It is not a public-facing app, and it is not offered to other users. Its only function is to upload pre-recorded video files (with captions and voiceover already embedded) to one TikTok account that we own and control.

Information we access

Through TikTok's API, this tool accesses only what is required to authenticate and publish content to our own account:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the connected TikTok account.
  • Basic account identifiers needed to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Upload/post status returned by TikTok after a video is published.

We do not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other TikTok user, viewer, or follower. We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond publishing our own content.

Information we store

Access tokens are stored locally in an encrypted/restricted-permission file on our own equipment, never shared with a third party, and used solely to authenticate API calls to TikTok on our behalf. Video files and captions are our own original content, drafted before upload.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate this tool and are deleted if the tool is decommissioned or TikTok access is revoked. We can disconnect this app from the TikTok account at any time via TikTok's own app permissions settings.

Changes
